# Science goal

What science does the astronaut do on this walk?

The astronaut looks for rocks that formed in ancient water on the Jezero Crater
west delta. They walk to 5 science stops. At each stop they look at the rock
layers and take photos and notes.

What the orbital data shows in our walking area (NASA CRISM, relative signal
strength from 0 to 1, not amounts of mineral):
- Carbonate: strong
- Olivine: strong
- Clay (Fe/Mg): weak here

Each square of ground gets a science score from 0 to 1. Our stops are the
places where the carbonate and olivine signals are highest.

Limit: these are CRISM browse layers (stretched images) from one orbital
observation. They show where a signal is stronger. They do not give amounts
and they are not proof.
